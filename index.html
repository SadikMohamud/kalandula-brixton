Our story

Nestled in the heart of Brixton, Kalandula brings the vibrant flavours of Angola to London. Named after the majestic Kalandula Falls, our kitchen is a celebration of Angolan Portuguese cooking.

Our chefs blend traditional Angolan recipes with modern technique, creating a meal that honours our heritage while embracing contemporary tastes.
